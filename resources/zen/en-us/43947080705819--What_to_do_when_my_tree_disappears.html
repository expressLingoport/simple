Troubleshooting tips for trees that have disappeared or look empty

The following are some suggested troubleshooting ideas if your tree has disappeared.

What to do if your family tree has disappeared

If your Family Tree looks empty or appears to have disappeared, do not worry—your data is usually still safe. The following explanations are common reasons why a tree might disappear and how to restore your tree.

Why does my tree look empty?

  1. Signed into a different account
    • If you accidentally created a new account, you will only see yourself as the home person.
      • Solution: Verify that you are signed into your original account. Check your email for usernames linked to your account.
  2. Browser or technical issues
    • Sometimes the tree does not load correctly.
      • Solution: Clear your browser cache and cookies or try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge).
  3. Relationships deleted or unlinked
    • If someone removed a relationship, your line may appear broken.
      • Solution: Check the Latest Changes on the person page to see if relationships were deleted. You can restore them.
  4. Deleted records or merges
    • If a person was deleted or merged incorrectly, you can restore them using their ID or undo the merge.

Steps to Restore Your Tree

  1. Confirm your account
    • Sign in with the correct username and password.
  2. Add living relatives until you reach a deceased ancestor
    • When you add a deceased person already in Family Tree, their ancestors will repopulate.
  3. Use the Latest Changes feature
    • Restore deleted relationships or individuals.
  4. Find by ID
    • If you know the person’s ID, use the “Find by ID” feature to restore them.
  5. Contact FamilySearch Support
    • If you suspect account issues, contact FamilySearch Support for assistance.
